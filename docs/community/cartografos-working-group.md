# Cartografos Working Group

The [Cartografos Working Group](https://github.com/cncf/cartografos) helps
adopters and end users navigate the CNCF landscape and the wider cloud native
ecosystem. Its best-known output is the
[Cloud Native Maturity Model](https://maturitymodel.cncf.io/), a practical guide
for organizations at any stage of cloud native adoption.

## Who should join

- Practitioners planning or leading cloud native adoption
- Architects mapping technology choices to organizational maturity
- Teams looking for structured guidance on people, process, policy, technology,
  and business outcomes

## How to participate

- Read the [Cloud Native Maturity Model](https://maturitymodel.cncf.io/)
- Explore the reference documents covering
  [people](https://github.com/cncf/cartografos/blob/main/reference/v4beta1/people.md),
  [process](https://github.com/cncf/cartografos/blob/main/reference/v4beta1/process.md),
  [policy](https://github.com/cncf/cartografos/blob/main/reference/v4beta1/policy.md),
  [technology](https://github.com/cncf/cartografos/blob/main/reference/v4beta1/technology.md),
  and
  [business outcomes](https://github.com/cncf/cartografos/blob/main/reference/v4beta1/business_outcomes.md)
- Join `#cartografos-wg` on [CNCF Slack](https://slack.cncf.io/)
- Visit the
  [CNCF community group](https://community.cncf.io/cncf-cartografos-working-group/)
