# Research User Group

The [CNCF Research User Group](https://github.com/cncf/research-user-group)
connects research organizations using cloud native technologies, with a focus on
Kubernetes workloads and the needs of scientific computing.

## Focus areas

- Research high-performance computing (HPC) and AI workloads on Kubernetes
- Scientific workflows and interactive compute environments
- Accelerators and big data for research
- Federated infrastructure spanning multiple research institutions
- Practices and feedback that help CNCF projects serve research workloads

The group focuses on research use cases rather than generic AI/ML platforms,
traditional non-cloud-native HPC, research data policy, or upstream feature
development. It can collaborate with the
[Kubernetes Batch Working Group](https://github.com/kubernetes/community/tree/master/wg-batch)
and the
[CNCF TAG Workloads Foundation](https://github.com/cncf/toc/tree/main/tags/tag-workloads-foundation).

## Who should join

- Researchers and research IT teams running scientific workloads
- Platform and infrastructure engineers at research organizations
- Architects working on shared or federated research infrastructure

## How to participate

- Visit the [group repository](https://github.com/cncf/research-user-group) for
  its organization index, resources, and updates.
- Join
  [`#ug-research` on CNCF Slack](https://cloud-native.slack.com/archives/CTW1NRLBW).
