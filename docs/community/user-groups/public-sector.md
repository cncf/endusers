# Public Sector User Group

The
[CNCF Public Sector User Group](https://github.com/cncf/public-sector-user-group)
is a community for organizations running cloud native technologies in
public-sector environments. Discussions cover compliance, procurement, security,
and operational patterns unique to government and public institutions.

## Who should join

- Teams building or operating cloud native platforms in government agencies
- Contractors and consultants supporting public-sector cloud native adoption
- Architects navigating compliance and procurement requirements

## How to participate

- Visit the
  [CNCF community page](https://community.cncf.io/public-sector-user-group/) for
  participation details
- Join `#public-sector-user-group` on [CNCF Slack](https://slack.cncf.io/)
- Browse resources and meeting information in the
  [GitHub repository](https://github.com/cncf/public-sector-user-group)

## Meeting cadence

The group meets biweekly on Wednesdays at 2:00 PM US/Eastern. Check the
community page or repository for the next meeting and agenda.
