# Platform Engineering User Group

The
[CNCF Platform Engineering Technical Community Group](https://community.cncf.io/platform-engineering-technical-community-group/)
focuses on platform engineering practices, tools, and patterns that enable
development teams to self-service their infrastructure needs while maintaining
consistency, security, and governance across the organization.

## Who should join

- Platform teams building internal developer platforms (IDPs)
- Practitioners defining paved roads, platform APIs, and service catalogs
- Engineering leaders adopting product-management practices for internal
  platforms

## How to participate

- Visit the
  [CNCF community page](https://community.cncf.io/platform-engineering-technical-community-group/)
  for meeting details and how to join
- Join `#platform-engineering` on [CNCF Slack](https://slack.cncf.io/)
- Contribute to ongoing initiatives such as the
  [Platform Engineering Maturity Model](https://www.cncf.io/blog/2023/11/20/announcing-the-platform-engineering-maturity-model/)
  and the
  [CNCF Platforms White Paper](https://github.com/cncf/tag-app-delivery/blob/main/platforms-whitepaper/latest/index.md)
