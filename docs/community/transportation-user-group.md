# Transportation User Group

The
[CNCF Transportation User Group](https://github.com/cncf/transportation-user-group)
is a community for transportation organizations using cloud native technologies.
The group connects practitioners working on connected vehicles, logistics
platforms, mobility services, and transportation infrastructure.

## Who should join

- Platform and software engineers at transportation and mobility companies
- Architects designing cloud native systems for vehicles, fleets, and logistics
- Engineering leaders modernizing transportation technology stacks

## How to participate

- Visit the
  [GitHub repository](https://github.com/cncf/transportation-user-group) for
  meeting details and resources
- Join `#transportation-user-group` on [CNCF Slack](https://slack.cncf.io/)
- Share your use cases and questions with the group
