# Research User Group

The [CNCF Research User Group](https://github.com/cncf/research-user-group)
brings together organizations applying cloud native technologies to research
computing. The group shares workloads, resources, and operational lessons from
scientific and high-performance computing environments.

## Who should join

- Research computing teams running cloud native workloads
- Architects and engineers supporting scientific, academic, or HPC platforms
- Organizations scaling data-intensive or distributed research applications

## How to participate

- Visit the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/cncf/research-user-group) for
  meeting details, resources, and the research workloads book
- Join the conversation on [CNCF Slack](https://slack.cncf.io/)
- Attend the group's calls on the first and third Wednesday of each month

## Meeting cadence

Calls are held on the first and third Wednesday of each month. See the
repository for the latest schedule, agenda, and connection details.
