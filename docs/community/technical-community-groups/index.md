CNCF Technical Community Groups (TCGs) are lightweight, topic- or domain-focused
groups that serve as a rallying point for community members to discuss, share
knowledge, and coordinate potential future initiatives. Unlike
[User Groups](../user-groups/index.md), a TCG is not aligned with a specific
industry vertical and does not carry the same formation/renewal requirements —
see the [Governance](../governance.md) document for the distinction between User
Groups, Initiatives, and Technical Community Groups.
