# Platform Engineering Technical Community Group

The
[CNCF Platform Engineering Technical Community Group (TCG)](https://community.cncf.io/)
focuses on platform engineering practices, tools, and patterns that help
development teams self-service their infrastructure needs while maintaining
consistency, security, and governance across an organization. It is a Technical
Community Group, not a User Group — see [Governance](../governance.md) for the
distinction between the two.

## Focus areas

- **Internal Developer Platforms (IDPs)** — platform architecture, service
  catalogs, platform APIs, and multi-tenancy.
- **Golden paths** — paved roads, reusable templates, and documented best
  practices.
- **Self-service capabilities** — environment provisioning, CI/CD integration,
  service dependencies, and built-in observability.
- **Platform as a product** — product thinking, user research, metrics, and
  feedback loops.
- **Developer experience** — portals, CLI tools, documentation, onboarding, and
  support models.
- **Operational practices** — GitOps, infrastructure as code, policy as code,
  security, and cost management.

## Who should join

- Practitioners building or operating internal developer platforms
- Architects designing self-service infrastructure and golden paths
- Engineering leaders measuring and improving developer experience

## How to participate

- Find the group's page and meeting schedule on
  [community.cncf.io](https://community.cncf.io/)
- Join `#platform-engineering` on [CNCF Slack](https://slack.cncf.io/)
- Share a platform architecture, case study, or reference the group's
  [maturity model and whitepaper work](https://community.cncf.io/)
