**July 28–30, 2026 · Yokohama, Japan.**

KubeCon + CloudNativeCon Japan 2026 brought adopters and technologists from
the open source and cloud native communities to Yokohama. CNCF-hosted
co-located events ran on July 28, followed by two days of keynotes, breakouts
and the Solutions Showcase on July 29–30.

- [KubeCon + CloudNativeCon Japan 2026](https://events.linuxfoundation.org/archive/2026/kubecon-cloudnativecon-japan/)
- [Schedule and session slides](https://events.linuxfoundation.org/archive/2026/kubecon-cloudnativecon-japan/program/schedule/)

Speakers who shared their slides have them attached to their sessions in the
schedule.

## Sessions from End User member organizations

The event published no end user track, so the sessions below are those whose
speakers listed a [CNCF End User member](/community/members) as their company
in the official schedule: Adobe, Airbnb, Ant Group (listed in the
[CNCF landscape](https://landscape.cncf.io/?group=members&view-mode=grid&members=end-user-member)
as Ant Financial), Apple, Cybozu, Intuit and LY Corporation.
