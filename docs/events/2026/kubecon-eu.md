**March 23–26, 2026 · Amsterdam, Netherlands.**

KubeCon + CloudNativeCon Europe 2026 brought adopters and technologists from
the open source and cloud native communities to Amsterdam. CNCF-hosted
co-located events ran on March 23, followed by three days of keynotes,
breakouts and the Solutions Showcase on March 24–26.

- [KubeCon + CloudNativeCon Europe 2026](https://events.linuxfoundation.org/kubecon-cloudnativecon-europe/)
- [Schedule](https://kccnceu2026.sched.com/)
- [Session recordings](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLj6h78yzYM2MXCOWSN9CqqID6OOvF7wxL)

## Sessions from end user organizations

The event published no end user track, so the sessions below are those whose
speakers listed an organization that meets the
[CNCF End User definition](https://www.cncf.io/enduser/#end-user-definition)
as their company in the official schedule: an organization that uses cloud
native technologies internally rather than selling or supporting them. They
include [CNCF End User members](/community/members) such as Adobe, Airbnb,
Ant Group, Apple, Bloomberg, Capital One, CERN, DKB, Fidelity, Intuit,
JPMorgan Chase, LY Corporation, Mercedes-Benz, Spotify, Thales, Uber and Ziax
alongside adopters from across Europe and beyond: banks and insurers such as
Allianz, Baloise, Banque Pictet, Millennium bcp, Monzo, OP Financial Group,
PostFinance, Rabobank, Raiffeisen Bank International and Saxo Bank; public
bodies such as the Belastingdienst, BWI, Insee, SNCF and Statistics Norway;
media, retail and travel companies such as Amadeus, Booking.com, Delivery
Hero, easyJet, Lidl, Skyscanner, Tchibo, The New York Times, Trivago and
Vinted; telecommunications operators such as Deutsche Telekom, Orange, SK
Telecom, Swisscom, Telefonica and Telstra; and technology, gaming and science
organizations such as Discord, Netflix, NTT, Pinterest, Preferred Networks,
Reddit, Shopify, Sony Interactive Entertainment, Ubisoft, Wayve, INFN,
Masaryk University and the University of Oxford, among others.
