**September 7–9, 2026 · Shanghai, China.**

KubeCon + CloudNativeCon + OpenInfra Summit + PyTorch Conference China 2026
brought the OpenInfra, cloud native and AI communities together in Shanghai.
Co-located events ran on September 7, followed by two days of keynotes,
breakouts and the Solutions Showcase on September 8–9, with practitioners,
technologists and community members from across the region collaborating on
Kubernetes, OpenStack, PyTorch and dozens of other open technologies.
