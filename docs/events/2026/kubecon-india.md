**June 17–19, 2026 · Mumbai, India.**

KubeCon + CloudNativeCon India 2026 brought adopters and technologists from
the open source and cloud native communities to Mumbai. CNCF-hosted co-located
events ran on June 17, followed by two days of keynotes, breakouts and the
Solutions Showcase on June 18–19.

- [KubeCon + CloudNativeCon India 2026](https://events.linuxfoundation.org/kubecon-cloudnativecon-india/)
- [Schedule](https://kccncind2026.sched.com/)
- [Session recordings](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLj6h78yzYM2PmaTdAzefPqjawTlop1lgG)

## Sessions from end user organizations

The event published no end user track, so the sessions below are those whose
speakers listed an organization that meets the
[CNCF End User definition](https://www.cncf.io/enduser/#end-user-definition)
as their company in the official schedule: an organization that uses cloud
native technologies internally rather than selling or supporting them. They
include [CNCF End User members](/community/members) such as Adobe, Fidelity,
Intuit and Walmart alongside adopters such as Aspora, AstraZeneca, Atlassian,
Autodesk, Circles, CommerceIQ, Expedia, Flipkart, Gojek, Highlevel,
JioHotstar, Krom Bank, Motorola Solutions, NPCI, PhysicsWallah, Rapido,
Razorpay, SailPoint, Sarvam, SBI, Shopify, Slice and Wells Fargo, plus a
student from Adi Shankara Institute of Engineering and Technology.
