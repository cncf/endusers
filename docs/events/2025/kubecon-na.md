**November 10–13, 2025 · Atlanta, Georgia.**

KubeCon + CloudNativeCon North America 2025 brought adopters and technologists
from the open source and cloud native communities to Atlanta. CNCF-hosted
co-located events ran on November 10, followed by three days of keynotes,
breakouts and the Solutions Showcase on November 11–13.

## Sessions from end user organizations

The event published no end user track, so the sessions below are those whose
speakers listed an organization that meets the
[CNCF End User definition](https://www.cncf.io/enduser/#end-user-definition)
as their company in the official schedule: an organization that uses cloud
native technologies internally rather than selling or supporting them. They
include [CNCF End User members](/community/members) such as Adobe, Airbnb,
Apple, Bloomberg, Capital One, eBay, G-Research, Guidewire, Intuit, JPMorgan
Chase, LinkedIn, LY Corporation, Royal Bank of Canada, Spotify, Uber, UBS and
Yahoo alongside adopters from across North America and beyond: banks, insurers
and fintechs such as Banco Mercantil, Beneva, Coinbase, CopperPoint, Credit
Karma, GEICO, ING, Itaú Unibanco, Juspay and State Farm; public bodies and
non-profits such as the American Red Cross, Child Rescue Coalition, Insee,
Open Healthcare Network and the United Nations; media, retail and consumer
companies such as ByteDance, Disney+ Hotstar, DoorDash, Egmont, Hinge, The
Home Depot, Mailchimp, Nike, Pinterest, TikTok and Whatnot; and technology,
gaming and research organizations such as CyberAgent, Electronic Arts, Ford,
GE HealthCare, Meta, Netflix, Niantic, NTT, OpenAI, Take-Two Interactive, The
Trade Desk, Zynga, Auburn University, Cornell University, TU Wien, UC Santa
Cruz, the University of Chicago and the University of Illinois, among others.
